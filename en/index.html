The SSH terminal and server toolkit
for Mac and iPhone

The terminal runs on the Ghostty engine. File management, port forwarding, keys, Parallel Run across hosts, and an AI assistant all live in one app, and your iPhone can even control your Mac. Open source under AGPL-3.0, no account required.

Download for macOS iPhone and iPad (Public Beta)

macOS 26+ (Apple silicon) · iOS / iPadOS 18+ · AGPL-3.0

Terminal

The terminal is drawn on the GPU by the Ghostty engine. Scrolling stays smooth even under heavy output, and full-screen programs like Claude Code and Codex look exactly as they do in Ghostty.

  • Tabs and Splits

    Drag a tab to split the view. Your layout comes back after a restart.

  • Terminals That Survive Restarts

    Local terminals are held by a separate process. If uTerm updates or quits unexpectedly, the programs in your terminals keep running and are there when you reopen it.

  • Automatic Reconnect

    On iPhone, your shell keeps running on the host. When you reconnect, uTerm picks it right back up and fills in the output you missed.

Files, Forwarding, Keys, and Batch Runs

The tools you reach for most when managing servers, right next to the terminal.

SFTP Window

A dedicated file window with tabs, drag-and-drop upload and download, and parallel transfers. Open files in apps on your Mac, and your changes go back to the host when you save. SQLite databases can open in uTable.

Preview and Edit

Open remote files in the workspace with syntax highlighting and Markdown and image previews. Edit, then press ⌘S to save back to the host. The terminal sits right below, in the same window.

Port Forwarding

Local, remote, and SOCKS forwarding. Rules for every host live in one panel, and can start automatically when uTerm launches.

Parallel Run Across Hosts

Run the same command on the hosts you choose, all at once, with each host's output in its own pane. Send it to the background if you like. When a host hits an error, hand it off to AI to keep troubleshooting.

Hosts and Groups

Group hosts by purpose, with support for jump hosts. Test the connection before you save, duplicate an existing host, or import from ~/.ssh/config.

Quick Commands

Save the commands you use most along with their target hosts. Run history is kept for 30 days. Or just describe what you want done, and let AI run it.

Keys

Generate or import Ed25519, RSA, and ECDSA keys, including passphrase-protected private keys. Keys are stored in your Keychain and sync through iCloud Keychain.

Known Host Fingerprints

uTerm checks the host fingerprint on first connection and blocks the connection if it ever changes. View and delete saved fingerprints here.

iPhone and iPad

The terminal, files, port forwarding, Quick Commands, and the AI assistant all work on iPhone and iPad, with hosts and keys synced through iCloud. On iPhone Duo, the layout switches to the large-screen design when unfolded or held in landscape.

  • Sessions Stay on the Host

    Your shell runs in a persistent process on the host. Switch to another app, change networks, or even have the app closed by the system: when you come back, uTerm reconnects and fills in the output you missed.

  • Keyboard Bar

    Gives you esc, tab, ctrl, and arrow keys, inserts common commands like claude and codex, and sends images and videos from your clipboard or photo library to AI programs on the host.

  • Dynamic Island and Background Running

    When Claude Code is waiting for approval, finishes, or hits an error, its status shows up in the Dynamic Island and on the Lock Screen. Touch and hold a tab and choose “Keep Running After You Leave” to stay connected while you use other apps.

  • Tabs and Splits

    Terminal and file tabs share one workspace and can be dragged into two columns. You can also search through scrollback.

Remote Control for Your Mac

Pick up the terminals open on your Mac right from your iPhone, or view and control your Mac's screen. Another Mac can be the controller, too.

Terminal MirroringNew terminals you open on your Mac show up on your phone automatically. Type from either side, with both screens matching cell for cell, and Claude Code's status stays in sync.
Remote DesktopHardware-encoded, low-latency, with audio. Switch displays and zoom up to 8×. For a Mac with no display attached, uTerm creates a virtual display sized to your phone's screen.
GesturesChoose trackpad or touchscreen mode. Swipe with three fingers to switch desktops or open Mission Control.
Remote UnlockEnter your password on your phone to unlock your Mac or get past system authentication dialogs. The password is stored only on your phone, and using it requires Face ID.
Files on Your MacBrowse, upload, and download files on a paired Mac without turning on SSH. You can also share a local folder with the Mac you're controlling, read-only by default.
Mac to MacWhen another Mac is the controller, the screen, terminals, and single-window viewing all work just the same.
Device GroupOnce your phone, tablet, and Mac join the same device group, any device in the group can approve a new one. No need to pair each device one by one.
Stays Connected Through UpdatesRemote Control runs in its own background process, so existing connections stay up when uTerm on your Mac updates or quits unexpectedly.

Window Mode

View just one window from your Mac. The Mac resizes that window to fit your phone's screen and its content reflows to match. When you exit, the window goes back to its original size.

  • Text Size

    “Sharpest” maps Mac pixels one-to-one to your phone’s. “Standard” shows text at the same size as on the Mac, and you can make it larger still.

  • Touchscreen Mode

    Tap right where you want. Drag with one finger to scroll, touch and hold then drag to move things, and tap with two fingers to right-click.

  • Independent Keyboard & Mouse

    Input goes straight to that window. The Mac's pointer doesn't move, the window works even when it's covered, and anyone sitting at the Mac can keep using their own keyboard and mouse at the same time.

  • Keep in Front

    macOS pauses drawing for windows that are covered. Turn this on to keep the window in front while you're viewing it.

Direct LAN Connection

On the same network, devices connect directly for the lowest latency.

Peer-to-Peer

On different networks, uTerm first tries hole punching for a direct connection, with no server in between.

Self-Hosted Relay

When a direct connection isn't possible, traffic goes through a relay you deploy yourself. The relay only forwards encrypted data and can't read what's inside.

AI Assistant

The assistant runs commands on the current host, reads the results, and decides what to do next. Every command runs only after it's approved at the safety level you've set.

  • Safety Levels

    Choose Confirm Every Step, Auto Read-Only, or AI Approves. Sensitive operations, and any command whose risk can't be judged, always need your approval.

  • Model Providers

    Bring your own API key to connect to Anthropic, OpenAI, or any compatible API, and choose the model and reasoning effort.

  • For Other AI Coding Tools

    In Settings, install the uTerm skill for Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, CodeBuddy, and Antigravity. These tools can then use your saved hosts through uTerm to run commands, transfer files, and open tunnels, without ever being given your passwords.

Privacy

uTerm doesn't run any servers that store your data.

No Account

Install it and start using it. No sign-up or sign-in.

No Data Collection

No analytics or tracking in the app. Your data stays on your devices and in your iCloud.

Keychain

FAQ

How do I install the iPhone and iPad version?

It's currently in public beta through TestFlight. Install TestFlight on your iPhone or iPad, then open the invitation link to join. New beta builds will arrive automatically after that.

How is it different from tools like Termius?

uTerm needs no account, and your data syncs through your own iCloud, never through third-party servers. It's a native app for Mac and iOS with a built-in AI assistant that can run commands, and it lets you control your Mac's terminals and screen from your phone.

Does it support Windows or Linux?

For now, uTerm runs only on macOS (Apple silicon) and iOS / iPadOS. There are no server-side requirements: any host you can log in to over SSH will work.

What do I need to control my Mac remotely?

Install uTerm on both your Mac and your phone. The first time you pair them, enter the verification code shown on your phone into your Mac. On the same local network they connect directly. Across networks, uTerm first tries a peer-to-peer connection, and if that fails, goes through a relay you deploy yourself (a single command installs it on your server).

Where does the AI assistant send my data?

Only to the model provider you set up, using your own API key. If you haven't set one up, nothing is sent.

Where can I report issues?

Please file them on GitHub Issues. For security issues, report privately as described in SECURITY.md.
